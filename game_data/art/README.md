# Music Life artwork

Original raster artwork generated for this build using the built-in image generation tool in generation mode (no reference images). Both files are 1536 × 1024 PNGs. The UI crops/scales them at runtime; source pixels were not edited afterward.

## shore-night.png

Prompt: Original production backdrop for an indie musician simulation, wide 1536 × 1024 composition. Painterly pixel aesthetic with restrained grain. New Jersey seaside music district at blue hour: wet asphalt, warm amber small brick club, parked van, boardwalk and Atlantic on the right, overhead wires, tiny silhouettes carrying instruments. Navy, teal, amber and cream palette. Negative space on the left for a title. Grounded and atmospheric. No logos, readable text, UI, or borders.

Used for the title and away-from-home menu sidebar.

## room-afternoon.png

Prompt: Original wide 1536 × 1024 painterly backdrop of a modest musician apartment. Acoustic guitar beside an armchair, notebook, four-track recorder, headphones, mugs, rumpled bed, vinyl crate. Amber desk lamp and teal window light; rowhouses in a seaside neighborhood outside. Grounded, intimate room with guitar and worktable toward the center/right, negative space in the upper left. No people, text, logos, or UI.

Used for the home menu sidebar. These are visual mood assets, not representations of simulated room inventory.
